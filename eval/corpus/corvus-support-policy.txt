Corvus Support and Service Level Policy
Meridian Systems - Internal Document CS-SUP-012, effective 2032-01-01

1. Scope

This policy covers support for the Corvus platform only. It does not cover the customer's
own Kestrel deployment beyond the agent's documented configuration surface, and it does not
cover custom CorvusQL authored by the customer.

2. Severity definitions

Sev-1: production ingest is stopped or data loss is occurring.
Sev-2: ingest or query is degraded but a workaround exists.
Sev-3: a defect with no production impact.
Sev-4: a question or documentation request.

3. First-response targets

The first-response target is the time from ticket creation to a human acknowledgement that
names an owner. Automated receipt mail does not count as a first response.

Bronze tier: Sev-1 next business day, Sev-2 two business days, Sev-3 five business days,
Sev-4 best effort.
Gold tier: Sev-1 one hour, Sev-2 four hours, Sev-3 one business day, Sev-4 three business
days.
Platinum tier: Sev-1 fifteen minutes, Sev-2 one hour, Sev-3 four hours, Sev-4 one business
day.

Platinum first-response targets apply 24x7. Gold targets apply during regional business
hours, 08:00 to 20:00 local time, Monday to Friday.

4. Update cadence and escalation

While a Sev-1 remains open, the owning engineer posts a status update every 30 minutes on
Platinum and every two hours on Gold. If two consecutive updates are missed the ticket is
escalated automatically to the Duty Manager, who must contact the customer within
15 minutes of the escalation firing.

A customer may request a manual escalation at any time through their account team. A manual
escalation raises the update cadence to the Platinum rate for the life of the ticket
regardless of the customer's tier.

5. Availability commitment and service credits

Meridian commits to 99.9 percent monthly availability of the Corvus ingest endpoint for Gold
and Platinum tenants. Availability is calculated per calendar month as accepted requests
divided by total well-formed requests.

If monthly availability falls below 99.9 percent, the tenant receives a service credit of
10 percent of that month's platform fee. If it falls below 99.0 percent the credit is
25 percent. Credits are capped at 25 percent of the monthly fee and must be claimed within
30 days of the end of the affected month. Bronze tenants receive no availability commitment
and no service credits.

6. Exclusions

Downtime caused by a customer exceeding a documented hard limit, by a scheduled maintenance
window announced at least 72 hours in advance, or by a force majeure event is excluded from
the availability calculation.
