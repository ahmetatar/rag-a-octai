The Roman Empire

The Roman Empire was one of the largest empires in ancient history. It was founded in 27 BC
when Augustus became the first Roman emperor, following the end of the Roman Republic.

At its greatest extent under the emperor Trajan in AD 117, the empire stretched from Britain
in the north to Egypt in the south, and from Spain in the west to Mesopotamia in the east.

The city of Rome was the capital and largest city of the empire. Roman engineers built an
extensive network of roads and aqueducts, many of which still survive today. The Colosseum,
completed in AD 80, could hold tens of thousands of spectators.

The Western Roman Empire fell in AD 476, while the Eastern Roman Empire, known as the
Byzantine Empire, continued for nearly another thousand years.
