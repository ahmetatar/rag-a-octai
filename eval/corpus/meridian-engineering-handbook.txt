Meridian Systems - Engineering Operations Handbook
Internal Document MS-OPS-003, revision 9, revised 2032-02-17

1. Environments

Corvus runs in three environments. The sandbox environment crv-sbx holds synthetic data only
and is reset every Sunday at 02:00 UTC. The staging environment crv-stg mirrors the
production topology at one tenth the capacity and holds anonymised data. The production
environment crv-prd is the only environment with customer data.

No engineer has standing write access to crv-prd. Write access is granted for four hours at
a time through a Portway request, which requires a second approver from a different team.
Read access to crv-prd logs is standing for members of the platform team.

2. Deployment windows

Deployments to crv-prd are permitted Monday to Thursday between 09:00 and 16:00 in the
deploying engineer's local time. There are no Friday deployments. A deployment freeze applies
from 20 December to 4 January and for the 72 hours before any announced customer event.

A change that is required to resolve an open Sev-1 may be deployed outside these windows with
verbal approval from the Duty Manager, recorded in the incident channel afterwards.

3. On-call

Corvus ingest is paged through the Nightwatch rotation. A Sev-1 page goes to the primary
on-call; if it is not acknowledged within 10 minutes it escalates to the secondary, and after
a further 10 minutes to the Duty Manager. Query-path alerts page the same rotation but with a
20-minute acknowledgement window.

An on-call shift runs from Wednesday 10:00 to the following Wednesday 10:00. An engineer may
not be scheduled for two consecutive shifts. Handover happens in a written handover note in
the rotation channel; a verbal handover alone is not sufficient.

4. Incident review

Every Sev-1 and every Sev-2 that breaches its first-response target gets a written review
within five working days. Reviews are blameless and are published to the whole engineering
organisation, not only to the team that owns the service. The review must record the customer
impact in the customer's terms, not in internal metrics.

Action items from a review carry an owner and a due date. An action item that slips its due
date twice is escalated to the engineering leadership review, which happens monthly.

5. Access requests and tooling

All access requests, including temporary crv-prd write access, go through Portway. Portway
requests expire automatically; there is no renewal, a new request must be filed. Requests for
access to the Tessera control plane additionally require sign-off from the platform team
lead.

Laptops are enrolled in the standard management profile before any repository access is
granted. Source code may not be cloned to an unenrolled machine, including personal machines
used during on-call.
